F1. SHOULD-FIX -- B1 (a) is not complete: an offer that the member's own group still reads as a reload.
    web/src/lib/media/groupSync.ts:285-296 (freshWhenAlone), groupMerge.ts:78-90 (mergeOffer), group.ts:129-133
    A group that shrank to one gets a package under a NEW signature key, while its state keeps the old leaf key.
    When that group grows again, `own` still carries the new key, and the member's first replaced merge offer is
    therefore NOT under its leaf's key. Its group takes it for a reload and the next committer swaps the leaf.
    Scenario (zzc2 T1, production timing, engine model on): Bob and Eve hold a group, Eve leaves the Talk, Carol and
    Dave join Bob. A listed lower id then shows a commit once a minute and never answers.
      shrunk:  5 s of 1200 s without a common key; 2 commits, 2 welcomes, 16 key packages. Trace: 15 s Bob offers
               (Carol knows that wire from his announcement, nothing happens); 75 s Bob's second offer, Carol
               commits remove+add for Bob and welcomes him; Bob cannot follow, starts over, offers at 80 s, Carol
               swaps again, Bob is back.
      control: the same room formed without the shrink: 0 s; 0 commits, 0 welcomes, 15 key packages.
    Bound: once per shrink-and-regrow (after the swap the leaf and the package agree), so it is no loop like the
    old B1; but "Bob and one other, the other leaves, more people join" is an everyday history, and any listed
    lower id can trigger the one start-over of that group's committer with a commit-shaped message.
    It makes two SECURITY.md sentences false: :826-827 "only a start-over or a reload makes a new signature key"
    (the next sentence itself names a third case) and :833-838 "The offer discards nothing and costs the member's
    group nothing ... the package is signed with the key of the member's own leaf".
    Fix: in mergeOffer, when `own`'s signature key is not the key of my leaf in `state`, make the offered package
    with the state's signature key (generateKeyPackageWithKey, as renewOwn does) before sending; or do that once
    when a group of one takes its first add. Add T1 as a test (zzc2.test.ts is ready to adapt).
