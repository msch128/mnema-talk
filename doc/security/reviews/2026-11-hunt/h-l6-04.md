## 1. Phase 2

**Repro.** I ran the finding's command at 6a481c744. The inverted known failure passes while the defect reproduces:


**Is it documented?** The strongest case that it is: doc/roles-and-permissions.md's Talks row at the base says "*View channels* + *Connect* to join". Read literally, that covers call Talks too. Three statements outweigh it:

- doc/talks.md, "Start a Talk from a direct message": "No lobby and no admin setup are needed, so it works on a fresh community". It also says the permission snapshot lets "only the conversation's members see and join it". It never says that View channels is needed.
- `OpenDMTalk` says "a community needs no setup for calls in conversations".
- SECURITY.md, "Who can read", and doc/architecture.md describe conversations as membership-based.

The server is also inconsistent with itself. `StartDMTalk` answers 201, rings the other members and posts an invitation card to a Talk that neither member can open. A design that required View channels would refuse the start. No test asserted the old behaviour as intended.

**Is it reachable?** Yes, through an ordinary setup: @everyone without View channels and channels opened by role. That is a common verification or onboarding gate. There is no attacker, and any member without such a role is affected.

**Duplicate?** No. Lanes 6 and 7 hold no other finding on how call Talks resolve.

**Security or functional?** Functional. The outcome fails closed: members lose access, and nobody gains any.

**Severity.** S3 under the brief: "wrong behaviour a user or operator can see". S4 does not fit: it needs no special rights, and the setup is common, not unusual.

**Verdict: STANDS (S3).**

## 2. What the fix changes (internal/perm/snapshot_channels.go)

In `channelPerms`, for a Talk in `dmTalks`, after the Owner shortcut (`All`) and the Administrator shortcut (`base`), the result is now `base | ViewChannel`. During the new-member speak wait, Connect, Speak and Video are removed (`dmCallMask`). Overwrites and the "no View channels clears ChannelScoped" step no longer apply to call Talks. The membership gate and the `dmTalkMask` in `Channel`, which removes Manage messages and Mention @all for everyone but the Owner, are unchanged. Timeouts and pending rules still apply, because `globalUnheld` already calls `silence`.

## 3. Does anything widen for someone outside the conversation?

No. `channelPerms` has exactly one caller: `Snapshot.Channel`, at internal/perm/snapshot_channels.go:83 (grep over internal/perm, non-test). `Channel` returns before that call in two cases:

- the channel is a conversation: `dmPerms`, membership only;
- the channel is a call Talk and the subject is not in `dms[dm]`: it returns 0, for the Owner and Administrators too.

The other snapshot functions that read overwrites do not apply to call Talks:

- `EveryoneCanView` excludes them through `IsPrivate`.
- `CategoryPerms`, `CategoryHeld` and `CategoryVisible` are per category, and call Talks have no category (talks.go:121-122 inserts `category_id NULL`).
- The view-as preview uses a made-up member who is never in a conversation.

Checked at run time:

- The fix's guard test shows 404 and an empty snapshot for a member with View channels, a moderator (Manage channels, Disconnect members, Manage messages, Manage Stages), an Administrator and the Owner.
- `TestNoCommunityRightOpensTheCallOfAConversation` adds allow-all overwrites for outsiders.
- My own reader spot-check (section 6): outsiders get no fan-out, no search hit, no mention, no read state and 404.

## 4. Does anything widen for a member beyond what the documents grant?

**Members who hold View channels server-wide: nothing changes.** At the base their result was `base`, then overwrites, then `silence`. No overwrite can exist on a call Talk:

- `SetOverwrites` refuses every temporary Talk (`refuseTempTalk`, internal/perm/store_overwrites_temp.go).
- A call Talk has no lobby to copy rules from and no category.

So the base also returned `base` minus the speak-wait mask.

**Members without View channels server-wide** now hold, in their own call:

- View channels;
- the channel-scoped bits of their server-wide set: Send messages, Attach files, Add reactions, Connect, Speak, Video, Disconnect members and Manage Stages (Manage messages and Mention @all stay masked).

Non-channel bits such as Manage channels or Manage roles were never cleared by the View rule, so they are unchanged.

Each channel-scoped bit against what it opens on a call Talk. I grepped every per-channel check outside internal/perm: `RequireChannel`, `Channel(...).Has`, `CheckChannel`. The bits checked per channel are View, Send, Attach, React, Manage messages, Mention @all, Connect, Speak, Video, Disconnect members, Manage Stages (ws/stage.go) and Manage events (event location, which checks View only).

- Send, Attach, React, Connect, Speak, Video: what doc/talks.md now grants ("what they hold server-wide").
- Disconnect members: granted on purpose and documented in talks.md and moderation.md. A removal from a call Talk is not audited (H-L6-03).
- Manage Stages: does nothing. Every Stage action requires `snap.IsStage(ch)` (ws/stage.go:173), and a call Talk is never a Stage. It is still not in the documented list. NOTE 1: mask it with `dmTalkMask` for tidiness, or name it.
- Manage messages, Mention @all: masked, unchanged.

There is no Move-members bit and no priority-speaker bit in internal/perm/perm.go, so nothing else exists to mask.

## 5. Members who lack rights, and the Owner or an Administrator inside the conversation

Scratch integration test, @everyone without View channels and without Connect:

- `POST /api/dms/{id}/talk` by a member without server-wide Connect answers 403 ("you may not join Talks in this community"). That member cannot start a call. This matches talks.md.
- With Connect from a role, the starter gets 201. The other member, without Connect, holds View, Send, Attach and React in the call, reads its chat (200), and cannot join: voice_join does not answer the ring. This matches the documents: what they may do is "what they hold server-wide".
- A timed-out member holds only View (plus non-scoped bits) in the call, and posting answers 403.
- Pending rules: `silence` covers them in the same way as a timeout. Checked by code and by the reference model's "rules pending" feature (200,000 communities), not by an HTTP test.
- A banned member of the conversation: a ban ends their sessions, so no request reaches the snapshot. Not tested.

`StartDMTalk` agrees with the resolution: both require server-wide Connect and respect the speak wait.

**Owner and Administrators inside the conversation.** Talks.md says "the Owner and Administrators included (like the conversation itself)". That means they get in only as members, which the code does.

- An Administrator member gets `All` minus `dmTalkMask`. That is unchanged and consistent with "nobody but the Owner moderates its chat".
- An Owner member gets `All`, including Manage messages in the call chat. That is unchanged by this fix. The conversation itself gives the Owner no moderation (`dmPerms`), and only the code comment on `dmTalkMask` says the Owner keeps it. NOTE 2: talks.md could say so, or the Owner could be masked like everyone else; the owner should decide.
- The Owner, as a member, learns nothing beyond being in the call, so this does not conflict with "who called whom".

## 7. The reference model, an independent rule, and the harness

The model's new `call()` is the code's structure, written again: `unheld | View`, then the speak-wait mask, then the chat mask. It is not independent. `compareCalls` checks only the six Talk bits against the model's `unheld`.

My own rule, written from doc/talks.md, doc/moderation.md (new-member waits, "joining or starting the call of a conversation ... follows the speak wait") and doc/roles-and-permissions.md only:

- Not a member: 0, for the Owner and Administrators too.
- The Owner as a member: everything.
- Any other member:
  - View channels;
  - plus Send, Attach, React, Connect, Speak, Video and Disconnect members, taken from the server-wide set (@everyone unless a bot, plus roles; Administrator means all);
  - minus the timeout/rules mask;
  - minus Connect, Speak and Video during the speak wait;
  - never Manage messages or Mention @all.

The server-wide set is computed straight from `State`, not through the model's helpers. Only `NewMemberWaits` comes from the snapshot.

- Compared with `Snapshot.Channel` on the channel-scoped bits for 20,000 generated communities: 49,619 questions, 19,687 of them for members. No disagreement except Manage Stages, held 11,483 times, which does nothing (NOTE 1).
- With the fix reverted, my rule fails at seed 4, the same seed as the author's model.

Random-community harness (visibility_random_dm_integration_test.go): before, a call whose starter could not see it was skipped silently. Now it is `t.Fatalf`. That is stricter. With the fix reverted it fails ("the mirror says the starter of call ... cannot see it", two calls in the smoke run). With the fix it passes.

## 8. New candidate finding (predates the fix, outside its scope)

A conversation member who holds Manage events can create a community event located in the conversation's call Talk:

- `validateEventTalk` (internal/communityevents/handler_validate.go:50-63) checks only `type = 'voice'` and View channels.
- The audit entry `event.talk_create` carries the call Talk's id and the event title as ChannelID and details.
- The Owner and Administrators who are not in the conversation read it, because privileged readers skip the class filter.

I checked this at run time with the default @everyone, so the fix is not the cause: "event in the call Talk: 201"; the Owner and an Administrator each read the call Talk's id and the title in the audit log.

This contradicts doc/talks.md: "Nothing about it reaches the audit log ... because that would tell the Owner who called whom". The fix only widens who can trigger it, to members without View channels who hold Manage events.

Suggested fix: refuse a call Talk as an event location (`talks.IsDMTalk`) or skip the audit for it. Suggested severity S3 or S4 (it needs Manage events). Proposed for the ledger as a new lane 6 finding; I have not written a finding file.
